Tessera Account & Data Deletion Request

Under Google Play user data policies, you have the right to request full deletion of your game account and all associated personal data.

How to Request Deletion:

  1. Send an email to teamdevgenius@gmail.com with the subject: "Tessera Account Deletion Request".
  2. Include your in-game player name or the Google email linked to your account.

Upon verification, all account records, save files, and inventory data hosted on our servers will be permanently expunged within 30 days.
