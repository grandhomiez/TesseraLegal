Privacy Policy for Tessera

DevGenius Studio | Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the Tessera video game application developed and operated by DevGenius Studio. This document comprehensively discloses how our application accesses, uses, stores, and shares Google user data.

1. Data Access & Information We Collect

Our application will access the data you have provided to us through Google Sign-In. Specifically, we access your Google Account email address, public profile information, and Google User ID (UID). We also collect in-game usernames, multiplayer session data, and crash diagnostic logs.

2. How We Use Your Data

We will use your data to provide you with the services you requested, such as authenticating your identity, maintaining your persistent game account, and synchronizing multiplayer state and leaderboards.

We limit our use of Google user data strictly to providing or improving user-facing features within Tessera.

3. Data Sharing and Transfers

We do not transfer or disclose your information to third parties for purposes other than the ones provided. We do not sell your data to third parties, data brokers, or information resellers.

Furthermore, we explicitly prohibit the use or transfer of Google user data for:

  • Targeted, personalized, or interest-based advertising.
  • Training generalized or non-personalized machine-learning (ML) or artificial intelligence (AI) models.

4. Data Protection Mechanisms

Security procedures are in place to protect the confidentiality of your data. We use encryption (HTTPS/TLS) to protect your information in transit between the application and our servers.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your authentication tokens and gameplay records only for as long as your account remains active. When the data retention period expires for a given type of data, or when an account is closed, we will delete or destroy it.

You may request for your data to be deleted by visiting our Account & Data Deletion Page or by emailing your request to teamdevgenius@gmail.com.
